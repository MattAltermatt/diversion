#338 · refracted sun on a pool floor

Slosh is how much of the spectrum stands rather than travels. A pool is a closed basin, so its long modes are seiches with nodes that stay put and the light breathes in place instead of marching across the floor. Drop the ripple far enough and the web stops focusing and dissolves into mottle — so “calm” is tempo, never ripple height. (This panel used to call that a “fold threshold”. It is not one: measured, the shipped default never folds at all — the web is pure density compression.)
