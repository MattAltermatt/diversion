1 — The ramp: thickness → colour

0 to 1500 nm left to right. The left edge going black is not a bug — it is Newton black film: below about 30 nm the two reflections cancel and the film reflects almost nothing. That is the physical event a draining bubble ends on, and it comes free from the same formula as the colours.

2 — A vertical film draining in a frame

The classic demonstration, and the honest test of the LUT: gravity thins the top, so thickness increases downward and the film reads as horizontal bands with black creeping down from the top. If this does not look like the photographs, the colour model is wrong and no amount of fluid simulation will save it.
