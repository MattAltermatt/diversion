#358 Lichen

colonies spreading over stone across decades

Mechanism in the issue: Colony = centre + radial profile r(θ) growing at a species rate with noisy lobing; where two fronts meet the older wins or a dark margin forms.

#349 Kolam

one continuous line drawn around a dot grid, then washed away

Mechanism in the issue: A dot lattice, then a single closed curve threaded between the dots on a fixed set of arc tiles; several loops merged into one Eulerian circuit, traced at pen speed.

#351 Asemic Script

an unseen pen writes flowing meaningless calligraphy

Mechanism in the issue: Glyphs are short chains of stroke primitives over a per-page invented alphabet; a pen model varies width with speed. Page fills, page turns.

#372 Girih

Persian strapwork stars laid tile by tile, then drawn stroke by stroke

Mechanism in the issue: Girih tiling by subdivision, then Hankin/Kaplan inference: two strapwork rays leave every edge midpoint at a fixed contact angle and extend until they meet.
