## Global Constraints

Every task's requirements implicitly include this section.

- **Slug** `lichen`, **kind** `'2d'`. A folder needs **both** `meta.ts` and `index.ts` or it silently vanishes from the gallery and 404s its route; `contract.test.ts` is the only thing that catches it. `index.ts` spreads `...meta`.
- **`dt` is MILLISECONDS**, clamped at 50. `dYears = dt / 60000 * yearsPerMinute`. Porting the probe's loop without converting runs it 1000× fast and **nothing catches it** — pure tests drive `step` with literals, and `diversionSmoke` only asserts a draw happened.
- **The host hands `kind:'2d'` a `size` in CSS pixels** and applies `setTransform(dpr, …)`. All grid arithmetic and the final `drawImage` use CSS px; `canvas.width` draws at 2× on retina.
- **Never `drawImage` a canvas onto itself** — the grid lives on a separate offscreen canvas.
- **No `Math.random` anywhere in the sim.** One seeded xorshift32 threaded through state.
- **`seed`**: `ui:'number'`, label `'Seed'`, `randomizeOnFreshLoad: true`, section `'Advanced'`, `collapsed: true`, and a **literal integer default** so `schema.parse({})` is deterministic.
- **No `background` field** (the sim paints every pixel; canon #256's sanctioned exception). `rock` and `sea` are the exposed colours.
- **Every `ui:'slider'` declares `min`, `max` AND `step`** — `diversionMeta.test.ts` requires `step`, and `Slider` otherwise falls back to 1.
- **Every control names its own inputs from `meta.label`** (`controls/accessibleName.test.tsx`).
- **Diversions never import each other.** Copy what is needed into this folder.
- `npm test`, `npx tsc -b --noEmit`, `npm run lint` green at every commit.

### Task 0: Noise, built on the FRAMEWORK rng — `noise.ts`

⚠️ **Do not hand-roll a PRNG or a hash.** `src/framework/rng.ts` is the canonical home,
imported by 164 files, and its header says diversions never own a private copy. The Global
Constraint "diversions never import each other" is about *diversion-to-diversion* imports and
does not apply to a framework module built to be imported.

This matters beyond tidiness. The probe hand-rolled `hash2` with `h ^ (h >> 16)` — an
**arithmetic** shift, which sign-extends, so bit 31 was always cleared and the hash returned
`[0, 0.5)` instead of `[0, 1)`. Everything downstream was half-scale, and it produced three
defects that were reported as three unrelated findings: storm survivors at **17.2%** against a
documented 8.5%, `lobe` spanning 0.62–0.81 instead of 0.62–1.00, and `relief`'s "ridges shed
water" half of the mechanic **not existing at all** (the field centred on 0.25, so the term was
always positive). `framework/rng.ts` uses `Math.imul` and `>>>` throughout and has none of this.

**Produces:** `src/diversions/lichen/noise.ts` — a thin *derivation*, not a copy:
- `makeRng(seed)` → re-export of `mulberry32`.
- `fbm2(noise3, x, y, oct)` → fractal sum over `makeNoise3D` at a fixed `z`, remapped from the
  framework's `[-1, 1]` to `[0, 1]`. That remap is the only new arithmetic, and it is the thing
  worth testing.

- [ ] **Failing test.** `fbm2` output is in `[0,1]` **and actually spans it** — assert the
  measured min < 0.05 and max > 0.95 over 200k samples, and that the mean is within 0.02 of 0.5.
  ⚠️ Asserting only "stays in `[0,1]`" is exactly the vacuous test that let the half-range hash
  survive: it passes for `() => 0.25`. Also assert continuity (`|f(x,y) − f(x+0.01,y)| < 0.05`)
  and determinism for a given seed.
- [ ] **Mutation:** **a constant-valued noise function must fail**, and so must one whose range
  is halved (`v => v * 0.5`). Those two mutations are the whole point of this task.
- [ ] Green; commit.

### Task 1: The rock — `rock.ts`  ⟵ **the arena decision happens HERE**

Two fields, both load-bearing: wetness sorts the species, sterile ground guarantees visible stone.

**Declare the arena first, because Tasks 1, 3, 4 and 7 all measure against it and two of them run
in subagents.** `ARENA_COLS = 250`, `ARENA_ROWS = 160` — the resolution every committed threshold
was measured at.

⚠️ **And decide the aspect policy, which no earlier draft named.** A fixed cell count plus a square
cell means letterboxing, which breaks the "the sim paints every pixel" premise the schema's missing
`background` field rests on. A fixed count plus a stretched cell makes thalli elliptical and the
contact margin 1px in x and 3px in y on a phone. The cited `salvage` precedent does **neither** — it
derives the *cell size* from the tighter side of a minimum arena and then fills the canvas, so its
cell count varies. **Ship salvage's actual behaviour**: derive the cell from the tighter side of
250×160, then `cols = floor(w/cell)`, `rows = floor(h/cell)`. Cells stay square, the canvas is
always full, and the cell count varies — which is exactly why founding and priming must be per-area
(Task 3), and why `lobe`'s wavelength is specified in cells rather than in fractions of the arena.

**Produces:**
- `interface Rock { w, h, relief, shade, wet, sterile, waterRow }`
- `buildRock(w, h, seed): Rock` — `waterRow = h * 0.88`, plus the **vein** and **speckle** ranking fields.
- `computeWet(rock, exposure, relief): void` — **in place**; these are live sliders and reallocating per input event is the rebuild-on-drag trap.
- `computeSterile(rock, bareRockPct, veinStylePct): void` — in place.

**Requirements:**
- `wet` falls off exponentially above `waterRow` over `reach = h * (0.10 + exposure * 0.52)`; 1 at or below the line; the relief term perturbs by at most ±0.2 and the result is clamped to `[0,1]`.
- `computeSterile` thresholds a blended vein/speckle field **against a 256-bin histogram** to hit the requested fraction, and **caps the marked count at the target**.
  ⚠️ **Not a fixed noise cutoff.** A mostly-zero field cannot supply the requested fraction, the threshold collapses to 0, and `f >= 0` marks the **entire rock** sterile. That shipped in the probe and was only caught by looking. The vein field must therefore rank *continuously* (`1 − vr`), not emit a mostly-zero mask.

- [ ] **Failing test.** `wet` monotone non-increasing up a column **in the row mean** (per-cell is false at default `relief`: the ±0.2 hold term dwarfs the ~0.016 per-row falloff); raising `exposure` raises wetness at a fixed mid-height row; `wet ∈ [0,1]` across `exposure × relief` extremes, asserted where the clamp actually binds (`relief: 1` on a hollow); `buildRock` pure in `(w,h,seed)`; `computeWet` idempotent (writes, never accumulates). **`computeSterile` lands within 1% of the requested fraction across 20 seeds at both style extremes, and at `bareRock: 0` marks exactly zero cells.**
- [ ] **Implement.**
- [ ] **Mutation** — all five verified to fail during Task 1, with one correction to this list:
  sign-flip on `hold`, removing the clamp, dropping `exposure` from `reach`, and hardcoding
  `reliefMid` to 0.5 all fail as expected.
  ⚠️ **"Reverting the vein field to a hard cutoff" is NOT a dangerous mutant** and it survives every
  test — measured, it leaves 73% of cells non-zero, which is far more than the 12% requested, so the
  histogram never collapses. That mutation only looked dangerous because the probe's half-range hash
  made the ridged field genuinely sparse. The mutant that matters is a **sparse ranking field**
  (cutoff at 0.012 → 5.4% non-zero), which puts **83% of sterile ground in a single band**.
  ⚠️ And it is only visible at **`veinStyle: 0`** — at the default 35 the speckle field dominates
  the blend and the broken implementation looks perfectly healthy. The dispersion assertion must
  therefore **sweep the style**, not test one blend.
- [ ] Green; commit.

### Task 2: Who lives where — `species.ts`

**Produces:** `SPECIES` (five, wet→dry, with `band` and `rate` from the spec table) and `habitability(sp, wet)`.

**Shape — a tent, not a plateau:** `0.72 + 0.28·(1 − |w−mid|/half)` inside the band (peak 1.0 at centre, 0.72 at the edges), then a **discontinuous step** to `max(0, 1 − d/0.11) · 0.55` outside, zero at `d = 0.11`.

- [ ] **Failing test.** Peak at each band's midpoint; the step at the band edge is real (0.72 inside vs 0.55 outside — assert both sides, which is a genuine check rather than a tautology); zero exactly at `d = 0.11`; **every species is the strict argmax at some wetness** (a species that is never optimal never appears on screen, and no colour test can see that); and **the argmax is monotone in wetness**, so bands cannot interleave.
- [ ] **Implement.**
- [ ] **Mutation:** swapping any two `band` entries fails monotonicity; widening `0.11` to `0.5` fails the reaches-zero test; flattening the tent to a constant fails the peak test.
- [ ] Green; commit.

### Task 3: The community — `colony.ts`

The keystone task.

**Produces:** `Colony { occ, age, scour }`, `makeColony(n)`, `prime(...)`, `step(colony, rock, params, dYears, rnd)`.

**Requirements** (constants are the probe's approved values):
- Growth: a **bare, non-sterile** cell adjacent to an occupied cell with `age >= 0.12` is taken with probability `k · rate · habitability · lobe · 1.35`, `k = min(0.9, dYears · 2.1)`, `lobe = 0.62 + 0.38·fbm(x/7, y/7, …, 2)`.
- Die-back: `stress = 1 − habitability`; fires above `stress > 0.46` with probability `k · stress² · 0.30`.
- **Founding is PER AREA**: `sporeRate · dYears · (cells/1000) · 0.10`. At 250×160 that is exactly
  the probe's `sporeRate · dYears · 4`, so the constant is calibrated by construction, not by hand.
- ⚠️ **`prime()` must be per-area too** — `Math.round(900 · cells/40000)`, not a literal 900.
  Measured: fixing only the per-step founding term still leaves **up to 39 points** of grid-size
  drift at year 20, because a flat 900 founders is 2.25% instant coverage at 40k cells and 0.56%
  at 160k. This is the single most likely thing to be implemented half-way. ⚠️ The probe used founders *per year* independent of cell count while growth is per area, so the founder:growth balance — which **is** the owner-approved look — moved with grid size (measured: 95% covered by year 40 at 40k cells, 65% at 160k).

- [ ] **Failing test.**
  - **Keystone: a LIVING cell is never overwritten in place.** Record `occ` each step over a long run; assert only `-1 → species` and `species → -1` transitions ever occur. ⚠️ Do **not** assert "a cell once taken is never reassigned" — die-back and storms return cells to bare and a different species may then claim them, so that statement is false and the test would either fail or have to disable die-back, at which point it guards nothing.
  - Growth never enters zero habitability, and never enters sterile ground.
  - A cell with `age < 0.12` cannot spread.
  - Determinism: same seed ⇒ identical `occ`.
  - Die-back is slow: from a fully-stressed uniform field, under 15% of cells die in one simulated year at default tempo.
  - **Founding is grid-size invariant at years 40 and 60** (measured: 4.3pt and 0.2pt once both
    founding and `prime` are per-area; without the fix, 39pt). ⚠️ Year 20 still drifts ~15pt and
    that is **expected, not a bug to chase**: the noise fields are authored in cells, so a wider
    arena has more feature periods across it and averages out the growth-rate variance — measured
    at 5.15× the covered cells for 4× the area. Assert years 40 and 60, and record the year-20
    residual with its cause so the next reader does not re-derive it.
- [ ] **Implement.**
- [ ] **Mutation:** dropping the `occ[i] !== -1` guard fails the keystone; removing the age gate fails; `0.30 → 3.0` fails slow-die-back; **reverting founding to per-year fails the grid-invariance test**.
- [ ] Green; commit.

### Task 4: Storms — `storm.ts`

A storm is an **event with duration**, not an instantaneous mask. Owner-directed; see Locked #4.

**Produces:** `NATURAL`, `WHIMSY`, `drawSeverity(rnd)`, `makeMask(kind, sev, rock, rnd) => { hit, cx, cy }`, `beginStorm(...)`, `stepStorm(storm, colony, dYears)`, `pickKind(whimsy, rnd)`.

**Requirements:**
- Severity `0.04 + u^2.4 · 0.92`. **~54%** fall under 0.25; median **0.214**. (Not 70% — an earlier draft said so, and the tempting "fix" is changing the exponent to ≈4.14, which would alter the distribution the second act depends on.)
- `beginStorm` collects struck cells once, assigns each a spiral phase from the mask centre (~2.6 revolutions, plus noise so the front is ragged not a clock hand), and **sorts by phase**, so `stepStorm` just advances a pointer — O(cells erased this step), not O(grid) per step.
- Duration `1.4 + sev · 4.2` simulated years. **~8.5% of struck lichen survives.**
- One storm at a time; the next one's clock starts when the current finishes.

- [ ] **Failing test.**
  - **Every mask in both families strips a non-trivial area** at `sev = 0.5` on 250×160, averaged over 20 placements: between 0.5% and 70%. Measured actuals are 3–46%. A mask matching nothing or everything is the failure mode and it is invisible on a screenshot of mostly-bare rock.
  - `pickKind(0, …)` never returns whimsy over 2000 draws; `pickKind(100, …)` always does; **and a mid value is checked statistically** — a mutant halving the probability passes both endpoints.
  - `drawSeverity`: **median** below 0.25 over 10,000 draws (a mean test passes for a uniform distribution), range spanning at least `[0.05, 0.9]`.
  - **The scrub**: a storm takes more than one `stepStorm` call to complete; survivors are non-zero and under 15%; a storm in progress blocks a new one; `scour` is marked on struck cells including already-bare ones.
  - `slab` has **ragged** edges. ⚠️ Assert this as a **paired comparison against the zero-jitter
    version of the same mask**, not as an absolute floor: measured, the jittered mask's exposed-edge
    count exceeds its own bounding-box perimeter by a mean of only 7.4% (2 of 10 seeds clear 20%),
    while the zero-jitter mutant scores exactly 0.0% every time — an unrotated jitter-free convex
    region is HV-convex, so its edge count equals its bbox perimeter by construction. The direction
    is robust; the ≥25% magnitude in the first draft was not reproducible under any interpretation.
- [ ] **Implement.**
- [ ] **Mutation:** zeroing the `slab` jitter fails raggedness; exponent `2.4 → 1.0` fails the median; making the scrub instantaneous fails the multi-step test; `SURVIVE → 0` fails the survivors test.
- [ ] Green; commit.

### Task 6: Render and wiring — `render.ts`, `meta.ts`, `index.ts`

**Requirements:**
- `paint(buf, colony, rock, cfg)` — pure given the buffer, no canvas, so it is testable.
- **Contact margins**: a cell touching a **different** species darkens by `margins` (default 50). Use the full 4-neighbourhood — checking only right/down puts the margin on one side of each border and it stops reading as a drawn line.
- Scoured rock renders as **darkened stone**, never tinted toward the sea's blue: tinting blue made a large low storm indistinguishable from risen water. Sterile ground renders as **warm pale quartz**, deliberately away from *Lecanora*'s `#b0b4ac`, or the two merge into one grey mass. Both constants carry that reasoning as a comment; no unit test can see either.
- `index.ts`: `setup` builds rock + colony and primes; `frame` steps then paints via an offscreen canvas scaled onto the display context in CSS px; `update` live-applies tempo, storm, whimsy, spore, margins and colours, recomputes `wet` in place for `exposure`/`relief` and `sterile` in place for `bareRock`/`veinStyle` — **without clearing living cells**, or a slider wipes lichen out under the cursor — and **returns false for `seed`**.
- **`resize(state, size, ctx)`**: the grid is a **fixed cell count** (`ARENA_COLS × ARENA_ROWS`, the shipped `salvage` pattern from #319), so a resize changes only the cell's on-screen size. Rebuild the offscreen canvas and repaint; carry occupancy, ages, `simYears`, the rng and the storm clock across untouched. Reallocating here is "the image dances".

- [ ] **Failing test.** `paint` writes every pixel (no alpha left at 0); a cell of species *k* renders nearer that species' colour than any other's; a scoured bare cell renders **darker** than the same cell unscoured and its blue channel is **not** raised relative to red; **a cell bordering a different species renders darker than the same species' interior, and at `margins: 0` it does not**; `update` returns false when `seed` changes and truthy when only `whimsy` changes; **`resize` preserves `occ` and `simYears`**.
- [ ] **Implement.**
- [ ] Green; commit.

### Task 7: The long run — `longrun.test.ts`

⚠️ **The first draft of this gate was wrong and the correction is measured.** It asserted that
coverage "drops below 60% at some point". Across eight seeds on the corrected harness, **three
never do** — and `seed` is `randomizeOnFreshLoad`, so that gate would describe a lucky rock
rather than the design. Pinning a seed hides the problem instead of fixing it.

What IS true across every seed measured: median coverage of colonisable rock sits at 97.9–99.4%,
the cliff is at or above 90% for ~85% of years, and churn runs at a median 0.22% of cells per
simulated year with a p90 of 2.76% — i.e. storms visibly disturb it and it visibly heals.

- [ ] **Failing test**, pinned seed, explicit `testTimeout`, headless, 400+ years, sampled every
  simulated year. Assert the properties that actually hold:
  1. Coverage exceeds 90% at some point (it does on every seed, by year ~250 at worst).
  2. **Churn is never zero over any 50-year window** — the piece never goes permanently static.
     This is the real "it has a second act" property and it is seed-robust.
  3. At least one storm strips more than 10% of the cliff within the run.
  4. Species shares are not frozen: at least one species' share moves by more than 3 points
     between its minimum and maximum after year 60.
- [ ] **Mutation:** disabling storms must fail assertions 2, 3 and 4. Removing survivors must
  **not** fail — survivors are a look decision, not a second-act mechanism, and a test that
  conflates the two would block the owner from tuning them.
- [ ] Budget: a 420-year run measured **~15 s** in plain Node. CI is slower and this repo has no
  global `testTimeout`; set it to the measured figure with a stated margin.
- [ ] Green; commit.

## What changed in Revision 1

Folded in from the spec panel and three owner-directed features, rather than stacked on top:
**per-area founding** with a fixed cell count and a grid-invariance regression test; a **`resize`
policy** (there was none); the **keystone corrected** from "never reassigned" (false) to "a living
cell is never overwritten in place"; **sterile ground** with the histogram-collapse trap written
down; **storms as timed spiral scrubs** with survivors, replacing the instant mask; **contact
margins** in render with a test that they vanish at 0; severity skew **54% not 70%**; `step` on
every slider; the species-order assertion; and a long-run gate rewritten to measure **colonisable**
cells, over 400+ years, sampled every year, on a pinned seed with a timeout.
