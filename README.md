# 🌀 Diversion

A personal web gallery of small, screensaver-like generative-art pieces — **diversions**. Each one is unrelated in content (a particle flow field today, a cellular automaton tomorrow), but they all share one framework and one design ethos. The framework owns the *chrome*; a diversion only has to draw.

## What's here

- **A framework** that gives every diversion two screens for free:
  - **Config screen** — controls on the left, a live preview on the right.
  - **Animation screen** — a full-viewport canvas (fullscreen-able) whose entire configuration lives in the URL, so any look is a shareable link.
- **Thirty-eight diversions:**
  - **Flow Field** (`kind: '2d'`) — particles drifting through a noise-driven vector field.
  - **Apollonian Gasket** (`kind: '2d'`) — mutually-tangent circles packed into every curvilinear gap by the **Descartes Circle Theorem** `(k₁+k₂+k₃+k₄)² = 2(k₁²+k₂²+k₃²+k₄²)`, recursing down to a self-similar fractal foam. A seed **quadruple** is placed once with the complex Descartes theorem (the only step needing a complex √); every child circle is then the exact *pollinate* reflection `k′ = 2(k₁+k₂+k₃) − k₄` of one circle through the other three, so the whole packing is generated with zero floating-point drift. Nine seed **families** (the irrational 3-fold-symmetric gasket plus integer quadruples like −1/2/2/3, −6/11/14/15) give structural variety across reseeds. The packing draws itself in coarse-first — one recursion **shell** at a time — then holds and reseeds; a slow rigid **rotation** costs one rotated `drawImage` of the baked buffer rather than re-stamping thousands of circles. **Style** (jewelled discs / line-art **rings** / **ink** silhouette) × **Colour by** (depth shells / size gradient), with a **Detail** knob for how fine the gaps fill. Two preset axes — **Look** (Stained Glass / Neon Foam / Etching / Molten / Relief) and **Motion** (Unfurl / Instant / Still / Slow Spin). Clean-room port of xscreensaver's `apollonian` by jwz (#56).
  - **Diffusion-Limited Aggregation** (`kind: '2d'`) — wandering dust freezes into a fractal dendrite: particles random-walk one at a time from a birth circle just outside the growing cluster and stick permanently the instant they touch it, so out of pure noise an intricate self-similar structure assembles itself — frost, coral, lightning. The exposed tips *screen* the interior (newcomers rarely reach inside), which is why it stays feathery and fractal; lowering **stickiness** lets particles rattle deeper before freezing → denser, chunkier growth, and a **drift** pull grows directional wind-swept dendrites. Seed from a **point** (radial frost star), a **ring** (hollow snowflake rosette) or a **line** (bilateral hedge). On-lattice: the occupancy grid doubles as the O(1) stick test *and* the arrival-time record that colours the growth as concentric rings, and adaptive long-jumps keep a far-away walker watchable. Every frozen grain is baked once into an accumulation buffer (only new grains stamped per frame) with a live-composited background + optional bloom; refills the frame then reseeds. Two preset axes — **Growth** (Frost Star / Dense Coral / Snowflake / Wind-swept / Spires) and **Look** (Frost / Ember / Lichen / Silver / Gilt) (#171).
  - **Abelian Sandpile** (`kind: '2d'`) — grains of sand pile onto a single point and topple whenever four stack up, each toppling scattering one grain to each neighbour; the cascade spreads outward and freezes into a self-similar fractal mandala with four-fold symmetry. Its namesake *abelian* property: the final pattern is identical no matter the order the topples happen in. A big central pile is dumped and then crystallizes over many frames — the avalanche front visibly sweeping outward, leaving clean fractal behind — before it holds, dissolves, and regrows. Grain counts 0–3 map to a four-color ramp so the nested structure reads (bulk-toppling on a fixed, viewport-independent grid; an offscreen buffer cover-fit to the screen). Two preset axes — **Arrangement** (Mandala / Twins / Trio / Cluster of colliding piles) and **Color** (Aurora / Ember / Lagoon / Copper / Mono) (#172).
  - **Morphogen** (`kind: 'webgl'`) — developmental biology as a screensaver: diffusing **morphogen** chemicals lay down smooth gradients from seeded sources, and each pixel reads its local concentration into a **fate**. The default is a soft-Voronoi **territory map** (argmax — which chemical wins here) with negotiating borders, distinct from Gray-Scott's Turing texture: this is *fate-from-gradient* positional information, so no pattern ever nucleates from empty field. A second `bands` readout thresholds a scalar gradient into stacked fates, giving the textbook figures as presets — **French Flag** (two opposing sources → a scale-invariant tricolor), **Eyespot** (concentric rings), **Segments** (parallel stripes). GPU ping-pong RGBA32F on the Gray-Scott substrate (small grid, `fwidth`-anti-aliased borders), with seeded source drift + birth/death so the body plan perpetually reorganizes. Two preset axes — **Body plan** (Territories / French Flag / Eyespot / Segments / Lush) and **Palette** (Butterfly / Savanna / Embryo / Coral Reef / French Flag) (#189).
  - **Differential Growth** (`kind: '2d'`) — a closed self-avoiding curve that slowly buckles into brain-coral / intestinal / lettuce-edge folds from purely local attraction (holds the strand together) + repulsion (buckles separate strands apart) + node insertion (grows length). An overdamped, position-based relaxation (no velocity → can't blow up) stepped on a fixed timestep for frame-rate-independent, seed-reproducible growth; a spatial hash keeps self-avoidance ~O(n). Grows to a complexity cap, breathes, then re-grows a fresh seeded world (a proper unattended-screensaver loop), staying framed under a slow-lerped autofit. Two preset axes — **Growth** (Coral / Cerebral / Kelp / Fine Weave) and **Style** (Bioluma / Ember Coral / Ink / Topographic / Porcelain) — over line / fill / membrane render styles and colour-by-age / sweep / curvature / depth (#144).
  - **Gravity Wells** (`kind: '2d'`) — particles with momentum falling through a field of transient gravity wells that appear and fade; orbits, slingshots, and push-pull churn.
  - **Plasma** (`kind: 'webgl'`) — domain-warped color fields, demoscene-style; the reference WebGL piece (proves the `webgl2` host path + context-loss recovery).
  - **Quasicrystal** (`kind: 'webgl'`) — a lattice of overlapping cosine wave-gratings, oriented at equally spaced angles, interferes into a shimmering *quasiperiodic* pattern — five-, seven-, or nine-fold symmetric, never exactly repeating. A shared phase drifts through every grating so the whole field slowly reorganizes, and a seeded base rotation makes each visit a different lattice. A single fullscreen fragment shader (no ping-pong), duotone crest/trough colouring. Two preset axes — **Symmetry** (Pentafoil / Snowflake / Heptafoil / Nonafoil / Dense) and **Color** (Aurora / Ink / Gold / Magma / Orchid) (#51).
  - **Julia Morph** (`kind: 'webgl'`) — the Julia set *z ← z² + c*, iterated per pixel and smooth-coloured by escape speed, with the constant *c* endlessly orbiting a circle in the complex plane so the fractal breathes between dendrites, spirals, stars, and scattered dust — never landing on one shape. A single fullscreen fragment shader; a seeded starting phase opens each visit on a different form. Two preset axes — **Family** (Tendrils / Spirals / Classic / Filigree / Scattered — the orbit radius) and **Color** (Gold / Ice / Ember / Orchid / Emerald) (#84).
  - **Metaballs** (`kind: 'webgl'`) — gooey lava-lamp blobs that rise, merge, and split; a CPU thermal-oscillator sim feeds a fragment-shader field sum.
  - **Substrate** (`kind: '2d'`) — cracks grow and branch at right angles into an organic network, each washing a soft watercolour cell beside it.
  - **Sand Stroke** (`kind: '2d'`) — grainy sand-painter strokes lay drifting bands of stratified colour.
  - **Squiral** (`kind: '2d'`) — worms wind themselves into tight square spirals that flood the screen with interlocking right-angled coils.
  - **Turmite** (`kind: '2d'`) — a generalized Langton's ant; ants turn by the colour beneath them, leaving emergent highways, spirals, and fractal growth (port of xscreensaver `ant`).
  - **Moire** (`kind: '2d'`) — concentric rings expand from drifting centers and interfere into shifting moire fields. Three styles: **Glow** (luminous additive rings), **Op-Art** (filled-disc XOR parity — the centers' rings merge into one bold 2-colour interference field), **Moire** (thin duotone rings that cancel where they cross).
  - **Logarithmic Circles** (`kind: 'webgl'`) — an endless, self-similar zoom through log-spaced rings of black-and-white circles; faithful op-art with a gallery color mode (port of xscreensaver `logarithmiccircles`).
  - **Strange Attractors** (`kind: '2d'`) — a long chaotic orbit of a Clifford or de Jong map painted as accumulated point-density; gossamer filamentary clouds that morph forever under always-on drift, the canvas itself the density accumulator.
  - **Demon** (`kind: '2d'`) — a cyclic cellular automaton on square, hexagon, or triangle grids; each cell is eaten by the next colour in a ring and rotating spiral "demons" self-organize out of pure noise (port of xscreensaver `demon`, after David Griffeath's cyclic CA).
  - **Game of Life** (`kind: '2d'`) — Conway's cellular automaton and four cousins (HighLife, Day/Night, Maze, Coral), rendered for the gallery: living cells are tinted by **age** (young → old along a colour ramp) and dying cells leave a soft fading **ghost** trail, so a stabilising board reads as glowing embers cooling to ash. Toroidal grid; when the population settles into still-lifes and oscillators — or after a long spell — it reseeds into fresh chaos. **Style** presets (Classic / Cloud / HighLife / Day-Night / Maze / Coral) pair a rule with a ghost length, over **Color** presets (Ember / Matrix / Ice / Orchid / Mono) (#182).
  - **Snowflake** (`kind: '2d'`) — a single frozen speck grows a six-fold ice crystal by **Clifford Reiter's** snow-growth cellular automaton: water vapour diffuses through the air and freezes onto the crystal's tips, and small differences in humidity, diffusion, and freezing rate sculpt dendrites, ferns, needles, or hexagonal plates — each flake unique, then it melts and grows anew. Runs on a hexagonal lattice (stored in a square array, drawn through a shear transform so the six-fold symmetry reads true), seed-jittered so every crystal differs. **Morphology** presets (Stellar / Fern / Plate / Needle / Sectored) over **Color** (Frost / Gold / Rose / Emerald / Mono) (#143).
  - **Physarum** (`kind: 'webgl'`) — a million slime-mold agents sense and follow a pheromone trail they themselves deposit; the field diffuses and decays into a constantly-rewiring transport network. All-GPU (agents in a float texture, trail in a ping-pong FBO); three morphologies — **Networks**, **Coral**, **Veins** (after Jones 2010 / Sage Jenson).
  - **Gray-Scott** (`kind: 'webgl'`) — two chemicals diffuse and react in ping-ponged float textures, growing coral, mitosis, maze, spots, and worm Turing patterns that never settle; all-GPU reaction-diffusion with the **feed**/**kill** knobs behind a named-pattern picker (after Pearson's classification of the Gray-Scott model).
  - **Excitable Media** (`kind: 'webgl'`) — a chemical dish self-organizes into a dense field of rotating **Belousov-Zhabotinsky spiral waves**: bright wavefronts chase their own recovering tails across a resting background, curling around defects into interlocking spirals and targets that never settle. All-GPU ping-pong on the Gray-Scott substrate, but an *excitable* cellular model — the Gerhardt-Schuster **hodgepodge machine** — rather than a Turing system: a cell rests until enough excited neighbours ignite it, climbs to a crest, then recovers, so the look is clean spiral waves, not coral. **Recovery ceiling** / **infection** / **contagion** / **wave-speed** knobs shape the regime behind a **Pattern** picker (Spirals / Broad Scrolls / Fine Waves / Targets / Turbulent) over a **Color** ramp (Ferroin / Aurora / Poison / Amethyst / Ink) (#135).
  - **Kuramoto** (`kind: 'webgl'`) — a field of coupled phase oscillators, each spinning at its own natural frequency, pulls itself into step: synchronized domains of one colour spread and collide, threaded by a dark web of **phase defects** — the point singularities where the whole colour wheel wraps around a core. Hue is each oscillator's phase (a cyclic wheel), dimmed by local coherence so synced regions glow and defects darken. All-GPU ping-pong (phase + cos/sin stored so the hue interpolates seamlessly across the 2π wrap). **Regime** presets (Spirals / Domains / Turbulent / Crystallize / Shimmer) balance coupling against frequency spread, over **Color** wheels (Aurora / Spectrum / Fire / Ocean / Pastel) (#137).
  - **Labyrinth** (`kind: 'webgl'`) — a slime-mold colony solves a maze: born gradually from the start corner, it buds forward at its own leading edge, climbs a faint chemical gradient toward the exit, and lights the shortest path before a fresh maze regenerates. All-GPU on the Physarum FBO host with maze gen + BFS solve on the CPU; **Behavior** presets (Veins/Tendrils/Seeker/Drift) and a **Goal pull** knob tune explore-vs-beeline (after Nakagaki 2000 / Adamatzky's chemo-attractant one-pass solver).
  - **Neural CA** (`kind: 'webgl'`) — a *learned* cellular automaton: every cell runs a tiny pretrained neural net over its hex neighbourhood, growing an endless, churning abstract texture from a seed. All-GPU inference (channel-tiled uint8 tensors, hex perception → two dense layers → stochastic update) with pretrained weights for **eleven** textures behind a picker (after Mordvintsev & Niklasson, _Self-Organising Textures_ / Hexells).
  - **Lenia** (`kind: 'webgl'`) — a *continuous* Game of Life: a single scalar field, seeded with smooth noise, is convolved each step with a ring kernel and squeezed through a bell-shaped growth function, condensing a primordial soup of glowing cells that swim, merge, and dissolve forever. All-GPU (RGBA32F ping-pong field, precomputed radial-kernel LUT); **Coral/Cells/Veins/Rings** pattern presets over a thin **μ/σ** growth band (after Bert Chan's _Lenia_).
  - **Langton's Loops** (`kind: '2d'`) — Christopher Langton's self-reproducing cellular automaton: a looped organism circulates an instruction tape, extends a construction arm, and buds off copies that colonise the plane into a coral of loops; when growth stalls it holds, fades, and reseeds. 8-state / von-Neumann / rotate4 (the canonical 219-rule table), with a sheath + signal-hue-ring palette and aged-coral dimming (port of xscreensaver `loop` by David Bagley, after Langton 1984).
  - **BoxCar2D** (`kind: '2d'`) — a genetic algorithm evolves little 2D cars across a procedurally-generated hilly track: each generation's population runs solo, and elitism + rank selection + subassembly-aware crossover + annealed mutation breed the next generation, so cars visibly improve into hill-climbers. Every trait is evolvable — free-form node truss, per-edge springs, and per-wheel radius / grip / motor / suspension / mass — with an informed prior (heavy chassis over light wheels) so cars can actually drive from the start. A fresh page load rolls a new seed (each visit is a different run); a share-link pins its seed and reproduces exactly. Two objectives — **distance** (go as far as possible on an endless track) or **time** (race to a finish line; fitness ranks by distance until cars can reach it, then by speed) — plus four terrain types (rolling / dunes / plateaus / ridges) and an ∞ track-lifespan option (one track, mastered forever). Time mode makes the race read at a glance — live 100 m split flashes against the record-holder, a finish-delta flash, a start→goal progress ribbon, a furthest-reached flag until the first finisher, and a translucent **ghost** of the fastest car replaying alongside the current one. A **champions panel** on the right edge lists the top cars this run as ranked mini-car thumbnails (a "hall of champions"), highlighting the running car whenever it's a carried-forward elite. An in-progress run **auto-saves and resumes** where you left off on a plain revisit (a share-link or explicit seed always starts fresh). Real Box2D v3 rigid-body physics (`phaser-box2d`, behind a typed seam) with motorized wheel joints, drawn as wireframes; a deterministic seed reproduces the whole multi-generation run (clean-room remake of BoxCar2D by Rafael Matsunaga).
  - **Flock vs Hunter** (`kind: '2d'`) — a shimmering flock and a handful of predators co-evolve. A murmuration self-organizes from a scattered spawn (boids steer by separation / alignment / cohesion + a fear of predators), while predators evolve their hunt (lead, lunge, fixation); both sides breed by a small float-gene GA each generation, so the selfish herd tightens and the hunters learn to lead in an endless Red-Queen arms race. Persistent bodies across generations and continuous respawn keep the flock always dense (it never blinks or empties); a fixed 1/60 sim in a toroidal world reproduces the whole run from a seed. HUD shows generation, survivor %, and the evolving flock + hunter strategy bars (co-evolution piece #159).
  - **Particle Life** (`kind: '2d'`) — colored particles obey a hidden per-species-pair attraction/repulsion matrix, and from random soup cell-like creatures, membranes, and endless chases self-organize. The matrix is *derived from the seed* (a **Feel** knob-set — asymmetric-vs-symmetric relationships + attraction bias — shapes its character), so every seed is a different world; species colors are spaced across a **Look** palette for contrast. Physics is the CodeParade/Tom Mohr *beta model* (a species-independent repulsion core keeps the broth from collapsing) over an O(n) zero-alloc toroidal spatial grid; luminous glow sprites + alpha-fade trails give the look. A fixed 1/60 sim reproduces the whole run from a seed (#133). The default palette is an old-school **Mariners** blue-and-gold ramp; a settled world (motion stopped) auto-reseeds into a fresh one, and the play screen's **📌 Copy this world** pins the exact seed as a shareable link (#191).
  - **Particle Life (GPU)** (`kind: 'webgpu'`) — the same seed-derived attraction matrix and *beta model* physics as **Particle Life**, run entirely on the GPU as a WGSL compute shader (two passes per step: all-pairs forces → integrate), so many thousands of particles swarm at once (8000 default, up to 25000 — a solid 60fps at the max on a modern GPU). A **World size** slider (1×–8×) grows the toroidal arena so structures spread out at the same count, and the view is a **zoom/pan camera** — scroll to zoom toward the cursor, drag to pan, double-click to reset — clamped to hard arena borders (no buffer outside the world). Instanced vertex-pulled quads render the two-layer glow (additive halos under opaque cores) into a persistent accumulation texture so trails work; **Feel** (Calm/Balanced/Lively) + **Look** preset groups mirror the CPU piece. The starting world is CPU-seeded — byte-identical to the CPU piece for a given seed — so a share-link reproduces the same genesis; only the per-step GPU evolution drifts by device. A **live interaction-matrix editor** exposes the hidden ruleset directly: an `n×n` heatmap (row = feeler, column = neighbour) you drag to retune each species' attraction/repulsion and watch the broth reorganize in real time — hover reads the relationship in plain language ("silver is drawn to navy +0.9"), and a hand-tuned matrix rides the share-link as a permanent Custom world (rerolling the Seed or changing Species rebuilds it; **Reset to seed** restores the derived table) (#204). The repo's first `kind: 'webgpu'` diversion: the framework acquires one shared page-level `GPUDevice`, and the piece follows the neural-ca ready-flag pattern for async device setup (#192).
  - **Swarmalators** (`kind: 'webgpu'`) — Particle Life's sibling: self-propelled particles that **swarm in space *and* sync in phase** at once, coupled (O'Keeffe–Hong–Strogatz 2017). Each particle carries a phase (an internal oscillator) and **colour is its phase**, so the emergent structure reads as a rainbow. Two knobs sweep the model's five collective states — a frozen **rainbow ring** (*static phase wave*), a shattering into oscillating colour clusters (*splintered*), and the default **active phase wave**, a living annulus whose colours slowly run around it — plus the two low-J disks (*static sync*, one colour; *static async*, all colours). Free-space, first-order, all-pairs on the GPU (two-pass forces → integrate, softened 1/r & 1/r² so fixed-step Euler stays stable); the swarm self-confines (centre of mass is conserved) so a fixed centred view shows every state, and a **Shimmer** knob gives each particle its own rhythm to liven the frozen states. Colour comes from a cyclic in-shader wheel (Spectrum/Sinebow/Pastel) — no per-species palette. Reuses the `particle-life-gpu` seam wholesale: shared device, ready-flag async setup, instanced glow + accumulation-texture trails, zoom/pan camera, seed-pin share-links (#215).
  - **Phyllotaxis** (`kind: '2d'`) — one local rule (floret *k* at angle `k·137.507°`, radius `c·√k`) grows the interlocking Fibonacci spirals of a sunflower seed head. A **Flow** motion emits shapes from the centre that stream outward off-screen; **Sweep** drifts the divergence angle so the spirals shatter into radial spikes and re-form; a **Spin** knob adds the hypnotic winding. Florets render as radial rounded **leaf** shapes (overlapping petals → a bloom of spikes → a folded-tile vortex) or a filled **Voronoi mesh** (`d3-delaunay`). Everything is a knob — leaf shape/size/shading, zoom, density, palette — with **Form** (Pompom/Bloom/Spiral Tiles/Mesh) and **Palette** (a **Faithful** rainbow-on-white matching the source video, a Cyan/Maroon vortex, Ink/Silver mono, …) preset groups. Emulates Milan Lajtoš's 2012 *Golden angle – Phyllotaxis* — a **partial** match; the exact centre-out growth feel is a follow-up (#184).
  - **Outbreak** (`kind: '2d'`) — a three-faction agent arena inside a procedural maze: armed **fighters** recruit nearby civilians into more fighters, while the **horde** bites them into more zombies, both draining a crowd of **civilians** — and whoever wins, it reseeds into a fresh outbreak. The arena is a **recursive-division maze** on one density knob, sweeping from a wide-open field with a couple of walls → vast plazas → a tight warren of corridors, dead ends and chokepoints just wide enough for a few abreast (connectivity guaranteed by construction; walls draw translucent so kills behind a corner stay visible). Agents navigate it by **flow field** — two multi-source BFS distance fields (zombies descend toward the nearest human to chase; civilians ascend away to flee; fighters descend to advance) — blended with line-of-sight direct-seek so they wind through corridors, funnel single-file through junctions, and corner fleeing prey in dead ends. Fighters fire only at zombies in clear line of sight and bullets stop at walls (no shooting through cover); a shot enrages the surrounding horde into a charge. Struct-of-arrays sim over a bounded spatial hash, fixed 1/60 step reproduces the whole run from a seed (#232).
  - **Doyle Spiral** (`kind: '2d'`) — a hexagonal packing of mutually-tangent circles whose radii scale by a fixed geometric ratio, coiling into logarithmic-spiral arms that zoom **forever in a seamless loxodromic flow** — circles born at the core, growing, drifting out along their arms, the loop pixel-identical because one full step is a symmetry of the packing. Built on **Robin Houston's Doyle-spiral numerics** (a 2-D Newton solve for the two complex generators `a`, `b` so three touching-circle radius-ratios agree), laid out over the lattice `aᵐ·bⁿ` with each circle's radius ∝ its distance from the origin. **Arms** (density) × **Twist** (winding) shape the spiral; **filled / rings / ink** styles × **radius / arm / angle** colouring (a baked 256-entry palette LUT keeps the moving hot path zero-allocation). Three preset axes — **Spiral** (Golden / Fine / Coil / Whirl / Bloom / Bold), **Look** (Gold / Neon / Ocean / Etching / Ink) and **Motion** (Drift / Still / Swift / Reverse). Fully deterministic — no RNG (#241).
  - **Hextrail** (`kind: '2d'`) — glowing arms grow outward along a hex lattice, each hexagon sprouting toward empty neighbours so a sparse branching tree of luminous 60°-angled trails spreads across the field, colour drifting stop-by-stop along the branches; when the lattice fills it holds, dissolves on a slow eased breath, and reseeds. Completed legs bake once into a DPR-scaled accumulation buffer (only the growing tips animate per frame) and a two-layer source-over halo+core glow keeps hue at the six-arm junctions instead of blowing to white; a global active-arm cap keeps the wavefront calm at any resolution. **Palette** (Neon / Ember / Aurora / Prism) and **Pace** (Zen / Bloom / Cascade) preset axes; a fixed-timestep, seed-reproducible sim (port of xscreensaver `hextrail` by jwz) (#108).

## Adding a diversion

Create `src/diversions/<slug>/` with an `index.ts` that default-exports a `Diversion`. It's auto-discovered by the registry (Vite glob) — no registration step.

```ts
import type { Diversion } from '../../framework/types'
import { z } from 'zod'

const schema = z.object({
  size: z.number().int().min(1).max(100).default(20)
    .meta({ ui: 'slider', min: 1, max: 100, step: 1, label: 'Size' }),
  // ...more fields. Each field's .meta({ ui, label, help, min, max, step, options })
  // drives BOTH the form control and the URL codec.
})

const myDiversion: Diversion<typeof schema._type> = {
  id: 'my-diversion',
  title: 'My Diversion',
  description: 'One-line gallery blurb.',
  kind: '2d',                                   // '2d' | 'webgl' | 'webgpu'
  schema,
  setup(ctx, config, size) { /* build state */ return state },
  frame(state, ctx, t, dt) { /* draw ONE frame; framework owns the loop */ },
  resize(state, size, ctx) { /* optional; ctx lets you repaint on reflow */ },
  update(state, config, size) { /* optional: apply config live, return true; */
                               /* falsy → framework re-runs setup */ return true },
  teardown(state) { /* optional */ },
}
export default myDiversion
```

**The framework owns the rAF loop** and gives you pause, tab-hidden auto-pause, offscreen-tile auto-pause, `prefers-reduced-motion` politeness (static first frame + opt-in), crisp `ResizeObserver` refit, an FPS readout, and fullscreen. You just draw a frame.

### The five UX invariants (non-negotiable)

1. **Readability is key** — legible type, no decorative-over-legible tradeoffs.
2. **Discoverable, not buried** — every param stays discoverable and carries help when its effect isn't obvious. Values needn't be on-screen at all times: collapsible sections, subpanels, and `showWhen` mode-swaps (e.g. the Color panel shows palette *or* gradient controls) are all fine, as long as the control is easy to find and its value returns when you open the section / switch the controlling field.
3. **Add help when confusing** — `.meta({ help })` renders as persistent inline subtext.
4. **Sliders only when bounded** — `ui:'slider'` needs `min`/`max`; open-ended numbers use `ui:'number'`.
5. **Err toward more contrast** — high-contrast palette, crisp borders.

## Control vocabulary

`slider` · `number` · `segmented` (enum) · `toggle` (bool) · `color` (swatch) · `colorList` (add/remove swatches, each with alpha) · `group` (nested, expanded). More get added when a diversion needs them.

Any field can carry `.meta({ showWhen: { field, equals } })` to render only when a sibling field holds a given value — used by Flow Field's Color panel to swap palette vs gradient controls by mode.

A diversion can also declare `presets?: PresetGroup[]` — named groups (independent axes) whose options each patch a subset of config. The config panel renders a dropdown per group above the form; picking one applies the patch through the normal config→URL path, and the dropdown shows "Custom" once a manual edit drifts off the preset. Flow Field ships **Flow** (motion) and **Color** (palette) groups.

## Credits / Inspiration

Some diversions reimplement algorithms pioneered by **Jared Tarbell** ([complexification.net](http://www.complexification.net/)) — e.g. _Sand Stroke_ and _[Substrate](http://www.complexification.net/gallery/machines/substrate/)_. These are independent clean-room reimplementations of the published algorithms, written from scratch in TypeScript — not ports of his source. Original work © Jared Tarbell.

Others reimplement classic [**xscreensaver**](https://www.jwz.org/xscreensaver/) hacks (© Jamie Zawinski and the original hack authors) — e.g. _Squiral_, after the `squiral` hack by **Jeff Epler** (1999). Again clean-room reimplementations of the mechanics, with the look upgraded to fit this gallery — not ports of the C source. _Demon_ reimplements the `demon` hack — **David Griffeath's cyclic cellular automaton** — on three grids with a gallery hue-ring. _Logarithmic Circles_ ports the `logarithmiccircles` hack, whose shader is **"B/W logarithmic circles II"** by **mrange** ([Shadertoy `mljcWR`](https://www.shadertoy.com/view/mljcWR), released **CC0**).

_Doyle Spiral_ is built on **Robin Houston's** Doyle-spiral numerics ([gist 6096562](https://gist.github.com/robinhouston/6096562), 2013) — a clean-room TypeScript reimplementation of the Newton solve for the generators; the loxodromic-flow presentation and rendering are original to this gallery. (Doyle spirals are due to **Peter Doyle**.)

_Neural CA_ runs the pretrained **Self-Organising Textures** neural cellular automata of **Alexander Mordvintsev & Eyvind Niklasson** ([Distill, 2021](https://distill.pub/selforg/2021/textures/)). Its weights (`models.json`) and the reference update math derive from the authors' open-source [**Hexells**](https://github.com/znah/hexells) project, used under the **Apache License 2.0** (see `src/diversions/neural-ca/HEXELLS-LICENSE.txt`); the WebGL2 engine is a clean-room TypeScript reimplementation of that pipeline.
